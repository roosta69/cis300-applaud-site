You are billed for each review that posts, and never more than the monthly cap you agreed to. Patients who would rather talk to you first are passed straight to your team, and they never cost you a cent.

Common questions

Is there a setup fee or a long contract?

No. You pay per posted review, and the monthly cap is a limit on your spending, not a commitment to spend it.

How is the monthly cap set?

We size it to your patient volume on a short demo call, so the cap matches how many patients your practice actually sees.

What counts as a posted review?

A review that a patient publishes on your Google Business Profile. If a request never turns into a posted review, it never shows up on your bill.

How do you protect patient privacy?

We sign a Business Associate Agreement with your practice before we receive any patient information, and we only use the contact details needed to send each request.
