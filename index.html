Why practices choose Applaud

  • Runs itself

    Applaud reaches out to the right patients at the right moment and handles the follow-up. Your team keeps doing what it already does.

  • Pay per result

    You are billed only when a review is actually posted. No retainers, no long contracts, no paying for effort that never lands.

  • HIPAA conscious

    Outreach is built around patient privacy from the start, so your reputation grows without putting protected information at risk.

How it works

  1. Connect. We securely link to the patient list you already keep.
  2. Invite. Applaud sends a warm, on-brand review request at the moment a visit ends well.
  3. Grow. New reviews land on your Google Business Profile, and you are billed only for the ones that post.
