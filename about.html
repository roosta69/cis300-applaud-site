Good care deserves to be heard.

Most patients who love their visit never say so online. Applaud exists to close that gap for medical practices, one patient at a time.

Why we started Applaud

Happy patients mean to leave a review, and then life gets busy. The practices that stand out online are often the ones that ask the most, not the ones that care the most. Being good was never the problem. Being quiet was.

Applaud was cofounded by Tom North to fix that. We reach out to the patients a practice already has, in a warm and personal voice, and we only get paid when a review actually posts. That keeps our incentives lined up with yours.

What we believe

  • Ask like a person

    A review request should feel like a note from your office, not a marketing blast. Every message is written to sound like your team.

  • Listen first

    Every patient deserves to be heard. When something felt off, their feedback comes straight to your practice so you can make it right.

  • Earn every dollar

    We bill per posted review, up to a monthly cap you agree on. If a review never posts, it never appears on your bill.

Privacy comes first

We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every practice before any patient information is shared. We use only the contact details needed to send each request.
